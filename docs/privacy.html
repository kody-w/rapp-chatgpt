Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026

RAPP Agent Builder is a ChatGPT app run by Wildhaven Homes LLC. This policy covers what happens to information the app receives.

What the app receives

Only what ChatGPT sends to the app's tools: a search phrase, an agent name, an operating system choice, or the text of an agent file to check. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, account or payment details.

What we do with it

We use it only to answer that request, in memory, and then discard it. We do not store it in a database, use it to train models, sell it, or share it, and we do not track you across sessions.

Service providers

The app runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP address and request time, to operate and protect its network, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Agent searches read the public RAPP Agent Registry on GitHub. Your use of ChatGPT is covered by OpenAI's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue.
